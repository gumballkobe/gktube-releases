Install it on a Fire TV

  1. Allow apps from outside the Amazon store (once). On the Fire TV, open Settings → My Fire TV → About and select your device name 7 times, until it says you’re a developer. Then open Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader.
  2. Get the free “Downloader” app from the Amazon store.
  3. In Downloader, type this link and select Go. GKTube TV installs:https://github.com/gumballkobe/gktube-releases/releases/download/tv/GKTube-TV.apk
  4. Open GKTube TV, go to Settings → Sign in with Google, and enter the code at google.com/device on your phone.

After that, GKTube TV updates itself.
