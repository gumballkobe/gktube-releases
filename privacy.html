Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version: GKTube TV has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics of its own. Your information stays on your TV and goes only to the services listed below, which the app needs in order to work. The developer never receives any of your data.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google gives GKTube TV access to your YouTube account (the “YouTube” permission). GKTube TV uses that access only to:

  • show your subscriptions and new videos from them;
  • show your playlists and liked videos;
  • like or dislike a video, and subscribe or unsubscribe, when you choose to on the TV.

GKTube TV never sees your Google password: you approve sign-in on Google’s own page (google.com/device). The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on your TV, inside the app’s private storage. It is used only to talk to Google and YouTube, and it is never shared with, sold to or sent to anyone else.

GKTube TV’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What stays on your TV

Your watch history, “Continue watching”, recent searches, chosen topics and settings are saved only on your TV, so the app can pick up where you left off. You can erase them at any time with Settings → Clear history on this TV, or by uninstalling the app.

Services GKTube TV connects to

  • YouTube and Google: to show videos, search, play videos and, if you sign in, your account’s subscriptions, playlists and likes. This uses YouTube API Services. By using GKTube TV you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net): when lyrics are on and you play music, the song’s title and artist are sent there to find synced lyrics.
  • GitHub: GKTube TV checks GitHub for new versions of the app and downloads updates from it.

Like any internet request, these services see your TV’s IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Removing access

Choose Settings → Signed in as … → Sign out on the TV. This deletes the token from your TV and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove GKTube TV’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

GKTube TV is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/gumballkobe/gktube-releases/issues.

GKTube TV is an independent app. It isn’t made by, endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.
